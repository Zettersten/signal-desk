# Signal Desk

HawkEye 360 strategists and business developers use Signal Desk to triage public competitive news about commercial RF analytics and adjacent Earth observation markets. A menu-bar or system-tray inbox gives them the latest ten records. The workspace turns selected evidence into a decision brief, with local organization, document export and explicit share handoffs. Sample records are illustrative, not current company reporting. No credentials are needed to evaluate the sample experience.
